Common questions

Does Nicety cost anything?

No. It is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and nothing locked away.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. Nicety has no account and never goes online, so it works anywhere, even in airplane mode.

Where are my saved cards?

On your iPhone, together with your reading history and settings. Nicety keeps nothing anywhere else, so deleting the app deletes them.

How do I turn the daily notification on or off?

In Nicety, open Settings, then Notification settings, and turn Daily discoveries on or off. The same page sets when it arrives, earlier or later, between 6 a.m. and 10 p.m.

You can also turn it off in the iPhone’s Settings, under Notifications, then Nicety. It comes at most once a day, with no sound or badge, and brings the day’s Discover card: a distinction or a passage from a book.

Is Nicety on the App Store?

Not yet. It is being tested with a small group through TestFlight, by invitation. The Nicety page will link to the App Store when it is there.

What about my privacy?

Nicety collects nothing. The privacy policy explains it in full.
