Nicety

Words & Nuance, for iPhone

The words just beyond everyday English, taught through passages from great books.

Coming soon to the App Store. Free, with no ads and nothing to buy.

Nicety is for readers who know everyday English well and want the words just beyond it: the exact word for a judgment, a feeling or a manner, and the difference between two words that almost mean the same thing.

Every word is taught through real writing. Each card pairs a word with a passage from a classic book, a short note on what the word is doing there, and the neighbors it is easily confused with. Distinctions set close words side by side (droll and witty; bloom, blossom and bud) and end with a simple test for choosing between them.

Four ways in

  • Discover: a word, distinction or passage to start each day, and as many more as you like.
  • Courses: a hundred short lessons, twelve cards each, on subjects such as wit, love and power.
  • Books: sixty classics, from Twelfth Night to Emma, read through their best passages, for the pleasure of reading first.
  • Topics: every word, grouped by subject, to browse at leisure.

Quiet by design

No quizzes, streaks, points or badges, and nothing to buy. Choose your own reading look: paper, color, one of three typefaces, and how the taught word is marked. An optional notification brings one distinction or passage a day.

There is no account to make, and Nicety never goes online. What you save and read stays on your iPhone. The privacy policy has the details.
