Privacy

Nicety privacy policy · Last updated 15 November 2026

Nicety collects nothing about you. It has no account, never goes online, and keeps everything on your iPhone.

This policy covers Nicety, the iPhone app made by Butterfly Farm Studio Limited (“we”). If you have a question about it, write to hello@butterfly.farm.

What Nicety does not do

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • The app makes no network requests. It works entirely offline.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting.
  • We receive nothing from the app, so we have nothing to sell or share.

What stays on your iPhone

The cards you save, your reading history and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. They never leave it through Nicety. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. If you turn them on, your iPhone first asks for your permission, and the notifications are then scheduled on the iPhone itself. Nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time in Nicety’s Settings, under Notification settings, or in the iPhone’s Settings.

Pronunciation

When you play a word’s pronunciation, Nicety uses the iPhone’s built-in voice, on the device.

Feedback

Nicety sends feedback only if you choose to. You can share a note through the iPhone’s share sheet, and you decide where it goes and what it says. If you send it to us, or write to us by email, we receive your message and the address it came from, and we use them only to read and answer it.

While Nicety is in testing

Before it reaches the App Store, Nicety is tested through Apple’s TestFlight, by invitation. TestFlight is run by Apple, not by Nicety. If you are a tester, Apple shares your name and email address with us, along with what TestFlight collects under its own terms, such as installs, crashes and any feedback you choose to send through it. We use this only to run the test. See Apple’s TestFlight terms and privacy policy for details.

The App Store

Apple handles downloads from the App Store under its own privacy policy. Nicety itself adds nothing to what Apple collects.

Children

Nicety is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If Nicety ever began to collect information, this page would say so before it did.
